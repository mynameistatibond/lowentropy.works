IU AI Studio — MVP Platform Prototype

This interactive prototype demonstrates a role-aware AI workspace designed for structured academic and institutional use.

The platform is organized around three integrated layers:

01
Task-Based AI Dashboard

Users land in a role-adaptive dashboard (Lecturer shown in prototype) that surfaces relevant quick actions such as quiz generation, document summarization, presentation building, and research insight extraction. The interface emphasizes repeatable workflows over open-ended prompting.

02
Document-Centric Knowledge Workspace

The Knowledge Base integrates AI directly into institutional documents. Users can upload, organize, filter, and batch-process files using structured actions such as summarization, quiz creation, rubric generation, translation, and media outputs. This enables AI to operate inside existing academic and legal materials rather than outside of them.

03
AI Literacy & Governance Layer

The AI Academy and Advisor surfaces provide curated tools, learning modules, compliance guidance, and policy-aware support. The advisor explicitly includes topics such as EU AI Act guidance, academic integrity, and disclosure practices — embedding governance directly into the product experience.

Interactive prototype

User Flow Overview

The MVP validates the end-to-end journey from entry to structured AI use.

The prototype demonstrates how a user moves through the system without needing to understand AI mechanics.

01
Login → Authenticated Entry

The user signs in and enters a persistent application shell with:

  • —global search
  • —role context
  • —notifications
  • —workspace navigation

The system immediately establishes identity and platform boundaries.

This is not a floating chatbot — it is an authenticated institutional workspace.

02
Role Selection → Context Definition

The user selects a functional role (e.g., Lecturer, Legal, HR, Admin, Support, Marketing). This determines:

  • —visible navigation areas
  • —dashboard quick actions
  • —task prioritization
  • —compliance context
  • —suggested resources

The system adapts to the job being performed, not the tool being used.

03
Dashboard → Task-Oriented Activation

Upon entering the dashboard, the user sees:

  • —a role-specific greeting
  • —a grid of Quick Actions
  • —recently used artifacts (prompt, tool, output, RAG document)
  • —announcements and compliance reminders
  • —AI Advisor entry surface

Instead of asking "What do you want to prompt?", the platform asks: "What are you trying to accomplish?"

This shifts AI from experimentation to execution.

04
Document Workspace → Structured AI Processing

From the dashboard or navigation, the user moves into the Knowledge Base. The workflow becomes document-centric:

  • —upload or select institutional files
  • —multi-select documents
  • —apply structured actions (Summarize, Quiz, Rubric, Translate, Slides, Lecture Script, etc.)
  • —batch processing hints
  • —file-type filtering (PDF, DOCX, TXT)
  • —RAG-enabled contextual use

AI operates on real institutional materials, not isolated prompts.

This validates integration into academic and legal workflows.

05
AI Advisor → Governance & Guidance Layer

If unsure how to proceed, the user opens the AI Advisor. The Advisor supports:

  • —tool recommendations
  • —EU AI Act guidance
  • —academic integrity questions
  • —AI ethics clarification
  • —practical how-to workflows

The platform embeds policy awareness and literacy directly into the experience rather than delegating it to separate documents.

Flow Summary
Login
→ Role Context
→ Task-Based Dashboard
→ Document-Oriented Execution
→ Governance & Advisor Support

This structure transforms AI from an abstract capability into an operational system embedded in daily academic work.
